7. Employee data and anonymity

Employees should understand what managers can and cannot see. By design:

  • Managers can see employees' names and roles, named messages, holiday requests, and which courses employees have completed.
  • Managers cannot see any individual's weekly mood check-in. They see only team averages and trends, and only once at least three people have responded.
  • Managers cannot see who sent an anonymous message. The database blocks that information from them.

To let a manager's reply reach the sender of an anonymous message, and to stop duplicate check-ins, our database keeps a private link between those items and the employee's record. Managers cannot see it. As the operator of the platform, Superb could technically access it, and we will not do so except where the law requires or to fix a fault, and we will not tell managers. We therefore do not describe anonymous messages as untraceable. Employees can delete their own data from the Superb Employee app at any time (see section 10).
