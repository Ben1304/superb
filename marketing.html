Everything in the base plan

Two apps, one workspace. Superb Manager shows you team-level wellbeing and feedback. Superb Employee gives each person a quick check-in and a voice.

😊

Weekly check-ins

Employees log their mood once a week. You see team averages and trends, never an individual's answer. Averages stay hidden until at least three people have responded.

✉️

Anonymous mail centre

Employees send feedback anonymously or with their name. You reply and the sender sees it, without you ever learning who they are.

🏆

Fitness challenges

Step challenges, cycling goals, mindfulness streaks. You set them up, your team joins in.

🔔

Alerts and a weekly summary

Notifications for urgent feedback and a weekly summary email to the manager, so small issues get seen early.

🔑

One-code joining

Your workspace has a team code. Employees enter it with their name and they are in. No IT involvement.

💚

Your own EAP details

Add your Employee Assistance Programme details so people know where to find confidential support.

Superb is a team-level wellbeing tool for managers. It is not a medical or counselling service and does not replace professional support.

A CRM your reps won't roll their eyes at

Reps keep their customers, meetings and follow-ups in the same app they use for their own check-ins. Managers see every rep and every customer in one place. No second login.

🤝

Customer profiles

Each customer has a profile with a timeline, notes, tasks and a health score.

📅

Meetings

Schedule video or in-person meetings and paste in your own Zoom, Teams or Google Meet link. Superb does not replace your video tool.

✨

AI-assisted meeting write-ups

The rep types their notes and AI drafts a summary and action items. The rep reviews and edits it before anything is saved.

📈

Opportunities pipeline

Track opportunities from identified through to won or lost, with an estimated value at each stage.

📊

Health scores

Calculated from how recently you spoke, the tone of recent meetings and open tasks, rather than typed in by hand.

🔎

Company look-up

Pull in public company details and recent news for a customer, to prepare before a call.

The CRM holds your customers' business contact details, so you are the data controller for it. Our Privacy Policy explains what we do with it on your behalf.
