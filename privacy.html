Privacy Policy

Superb Wellness · Scotland, United Kingdom
Last updated 13 October 2026

We collect only what is needed to run Superb. Managers can never see an individual employee's mood or who sent an anonymous message — the database itself blocks it. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising. Employees can delete their own data in the app, and managers can export or delete their workspace. Please read section 4 for the honest detail on what "anonymous" means here.

1. Who we are

Superb Wellness ("Superb", "we", "us") operates the Superb Manager and Superb Employee applications and the website at superbwellness.co.uk. We are based in Scotland and subject to UK data protection law, including UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

For information about employees and managers, the employer is the data controller for how it uses Superb with its staff, and Superb is the processor. For manager account details, billing, and our own security and service operations, Superb is the controller. For customer information entered into the CRM add-on, the employer is the controller and Superb is the processor. Questions: bengraybaikie@hotmail.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Manager account data — company name, manager name, email address and password (stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider).
  • Employee data — name, role, and a workspace membership. Employees do not give an email address. Joining creates an anonymous sign-in identifier used to keep them logged in.
  • Check-ins — a weekly mood score and optional note. Stored against the employee's record only to prevent duplicates. Managers see team-level averages only.
  • Feedback messages — messages sent through the mail centre, and replies. Named messages show the sender's name. Anonymous messages are shown to managers without any sender (see section 4).
  • Other workspace content — fitness challenge entries, recognition, tasks, one-to-one notes, holiday requests (dates and optional reason), course progress, and any policies or course material the manager uploads, depending on the features in use.
  • CRM data (add-on) — customers' company and contact details, meeting notes and summaries, tasks and opportunities entered by reps. This is business contact information about third parties and is controlled by your employer.
  • AI inputs (add-ons) — see section 7.
  • Billing data — billing name, email and plan. Card details are handled entirely by Stripe and never reach our servers; we hold a Stripe customer and subscription reference and the subscription status.
  • Security and usage records — records of failed attempts to join with a team code (used to block guessing), counters of daily AI and look-up use (used to cap usage), and standard hosting logs. We do not use advertising or behavioural analytics.

3. How we use your data

We use data to run the platform; show managers team-level wellbeing information without identifying individuals; process payments and manage subscriptions; send service emails (such as the manager's weekly summary and urgent-feedback alerts, if switched on, and warnings before a locked workspace is deleted); keep the service secure and prevent abuse; and meet legal obligations. Employees do not have email addresses with us, so we do not email them.

We do not use personal data for advertising and we do not sell it.

4. Anonymity and employee privacy — what is and is not hidden

Protecting employee privacy is built into how the database is set up, not only into what the screens show:

  • Moods. Managers have no access to individual check-ins. They receive only team averages and trends, and these stay hidden until at least three people have responded.
  • Anonymous messages. Managers see anonymous messages with the sender blank, and cannot look up who sent them.
  • Replies. So that a manager's reply can reach the right person, and so a person cannot submit two check-ins in a week, the system keeps a private reference to the employee against those items. Managers cannot read it.
  • Our access. Because that reference exists, someone with administrative access to the database (that is, Superb as the operator, or our hosting provider) could technically connect an anonymous message or check-in to an employee. We do not do this, and will not disclose it to managers, except where required by law or to fix a fault. For that reason we say anonymous to managers, not untraceable.
  • What managers can see. Employee names and roles, named messages, holiday requests, and course completion.

Employees can delete their own data from the Superb Employee app at any time. This removes their check-ins, messages, notifications, membership and sign-in record.

5. Legal basis for processing

For our own processing we rely on contract (delivering the service the manager subscribed to), legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention and improving reliability, where these do not override your rights), legal obligation (such as tax records) and consent where we ask for it. Employers must decide and document their own lawful basis for using Superb with their staff and customers.

6. Retention

  • We keep workspace data while the workspace is active.
  • Managers can export a copy of their data and delete their workspace in the app. Deletion is permanent. Employees can delete their own data.
  • If a workspace stays locked (trial ended with no subscription, or subscription cancelled), we email the manager a warning at about 76 days and permanently delete the workspace and all its data at 90 days of being locked.
  • Backups. Our database provider keeps rolling backups for a short period (currently around 7 days). Deleted data may sit in those backups until they expire, and is not restored except to recover from a fault.
  • Billing and tax records, held by us and Stripe, are kept for as long as the law requires (currently up to seven years).
  • Records of failed join attempts and usage counters are kept only briefly for security and are removed when the workspace is deleted.

7. Who we share data with

We use the following providers to run Superb. We have, or will have in place before launch, data processing terms with each:

  • Supabase — database hosting and authentication. Our project is hosted in the UK or EU.
  • Vercel — hosting of the website and apps (served from a global network).
  • Stripe — payments and subscription management.
  • Resend — sending service emails to managers (the manager's email address and counts such as unanswered messages or the team mood average; emails never contain the text of a message or who sent it).
  • Anthropic — used only if the AI features are in use. The CRM meeting write-up helper sends the rep's typed notes and the customer's name. AI Wellbeing Guides send only a fixed topic name and no personal data.
  • Companies House and a news data provider — used for the CRM company look-up. Only a company name or registered number is sent.

We do not share personal data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

8. International transfers

Some of the providers above (for example Stripe, Vercel, Resend and Anthropic) may process data outside the UK and EU, including in the United States. Where they do, we rely on safeguards the law recognises, such as the UK's International Data Transfer Addendum or an adequacy arrangement, as set out in each provider's data processing terms.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and encrypted at rest by our hosting provider. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Database rules restrict each company to its own data and restrict each role to what it needs. We limit sign-up and join-code guessing, cap input sizes, and limit usage of paid AI features. No online service can promise perfect security. If a breach affects personal data we will tell affected employers and, where required, the ICO.

10. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to access your personal data, correct it, have it erased, receive it in a portable format, object to certain processing, or ask us to restrict it. Employees can erase their own data in the app; managers can export their workspace data in the app. For employee data held by an employer, please contact your employer first, as they are the controller; we will help them respond.

For anything else, email bengraybaikie@hotmail.com. We aim to respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

11. Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies and we do not use third-party analytics. The apps store a sign-in session and a few settings in your browser's local storage, which is necessary to keep you logged in and to run the service. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when a page loads.

12. Children

Superb is a business tool for workplaces. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you think a person under 16 has given us data, tell us and we will remove it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change is material, we will email account holders at least 14 days before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
